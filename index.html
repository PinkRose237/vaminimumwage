The rate, step by step

Virginia law requires employers to pay the greater of the state rate or the federal minimum ($7.25 since 2009). The 2026 legislation, signed April 2026, fixed the 2027 and 2028 steps in statute and moved annual inflation indexing to start in 2029.

The 2029 mechanism

How Virginia will set the 2029 wage

§ 40.1-28.10(F), paraphrased with key phrases quoted “By October 1, 2028, and annually thereafter, the Commissioner shall establish the adjusted state hourly minimum wage” that applies to the 12 months starting the following January 1. The new rate equals the current state rate plus a percentage equal to the change in the average Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) over the most recent calendar year for which data is available. Each annual adjustment cannot be less than zero.
Wage2029 = $15.00 × (1 + ΔCPI-U2027 avg vs 2026 avg)
e.g. $15.00 × 1.026 = $15.39
  • The Commissioner of Labor and Industry (Virginia DOLI) does the calculation and publishes the rate. The General Assembly does not vote on it.
  • The index is national CPI-U, published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. It is not a Virginia or South-region index.
  • The rate can never go down. If prices fall, the adjustment is zero and the rate holds.
  • Rounding is not specified in the statute. DOLI has rounded to the nearest cent ($12.41 × 1.029 = $12.77). This forecast does the same each year.
  • Two-year lag. Because the rate is fixed by October 1, the latest full year of data is the year before. The 2029 rate reflects 2027 prices, the 2030 rate reflects 2028, and so on.
  • Average versus December. For the 2025 and 2026 increases, DOLI used the December-to-December change (3.4% for 2023 and 2.9% for 2024). The amended text says “average,” which points to the annual-average change. For 2027 the two measures are expected to be within about 0.1 point of each other.

  1. Jan 1, 2027Statutory step to $13.75.

  3. Jan 1, 2028Statutory step to $15.00. This is the base for indexing.

  5. By Oct 1, 2028DOLI Commissioner announces the adjusted 2029 rate.
  6. Jan 1, 2029First inflation-adjusted rate takes effect. The cycle then repeats every year.

Price trend for 2027: the August 2026 Survey of Professional Forecasters (Q3) puts 2027 headline CPI near 2.5%. CPI-U ran hot in spring 2026 (3.4% year over year in August), and that higher 2026 level carries into the 2027 annual average. The base estimate is 2.6%, or about $15.39. A 2.3% to 3.0% range gives $15.35 to $15.45.

Method and sources

How the forecast is built

  • Recurrence: Ratey = round(Ratey−1 × (1 + max(0, CPIy−2)), cents) for every year from 2029, starting from $15.00 in 2028.
  • Base (2.6% then 2.3%): The 2.3% long-run rate matches the Philadelphia Fed SPF 10-year headline CPI expectation (2.30% for 2026–2035) and CBO’s CPI path of 2.3% from 2028 onward. The 2027 figure adds the carry-over from 2026’s price jump to the SPF 2027 forecast.
  • Low (2.3% then 2.0%): CPI sits at roughly the Federal Reserve’s 2% target. CPI usually runs a few tenths above the Fed’s preferred PCE gauge, so this is a deliberately cool path.
  • High (3.0% then 3.0%): Inflation stays near its 2025–26 pace. That is about 0.4 point above the 1990–2025 CPI-U average of roughly 2.6%.
  • Not modeled: future changes to the law, a higher federal minimum, regional or local wages (Virginia has none), and tipped-wage rules. Projections decades out depend entirely on the inflation assumption. Treat them as illustrations of the indexing formula, not predictions of policy.
