Virginia law requires employers to pay the greater of the state rate or the federal minimum ($7.25 since 2009). The 2026 legislation, signed in April 2026, fixed the 2027 and 2028 steps in statute. Annual inflation indexing starts in 2029.

The 2029 mechanism

How Virginia will set the 2029 wage

§ 40.1-28.10(F), summarized with key phrases quoted “By October 1, 2028, and annually thereafter, the Commissioner shall establish the adjusted state hourly minimum wage” for the 12 months starting the following January 1. The new rate is the current rate plus the percentage by which the U.S. city average CPI-U “has increased during the most recent calendar year for which such information is available.” Each adjustment cannot be less than zero.
Wage2029 = $15.00 × (1 + CPI-U change, Dec 2026 → Dec 2027)
Today’s estimate: $15.00 × (1 + {{FIRST_CPI_PCT}}) = {{EST_FIRST}}
  • Who sets it: the Commissioner of Labor and Industry (Virginia DOLI) calculates and publishes the rate. The General Assembly does not vote on it.
  • Which index: national CPI-U (U.S. city average, all items) from the Bureau of Labor Statistics. Not a Virginia or regional index.
  • Which change: DOLI has applied “increased during the most recent calendar year” as the December-to-December change. It used 3.4% for 2023 and 2.9% for 2024. This forecast does the same.
  • Never lower: if prices fall, the adjustment is zero and the rate holds.
  • Rounding: the statute is silent. DOLI rounds to the nearest cent ($12.41 × 1.029 = $12.77), and so does this forecast, every year.
  • Two-year lag: the rate is set by October 1, so the latest full year of data is the year before. The 2029 rate reflects 2027 prices, 2030 reflects 2028, and so on.

Calendar to the first indexed rate

  1. Jan 1, 2027Statutory step to $13.75.

  5. By Oct 1, 2028DOLI announces the 2029 rate. In 2025 it announced in late July.
  6. Jan 1, 2029The first inflation-adjusted rate takes effect. The cycle repeats every year.

Method and sources

How the forecast is built and kept current

  • Recurrence: Ratey = round(Ratey−1 × (1 + max(0, CPIy−2)), cents) for every year not yet set by law or by DOLI.
  • CPI data: once both Decembers are published, the actual change is used and the year is marked “Calculated”. For a year in progress, the seasonally adjusted index so far is extended to December at the assumed rate.
  • Base: {{BASE_TEXT}}
  • Low and High: the base near-term outlook minus 0.5 or plus 0.7 points, then 2.0% (about the Fed’s target) or 3.0% a year over the long run.
  • Automatic updates: a scheduled GitHub Action pulls new CPI data each month after the BLS release. Each week (daily during the legislative session) it checks the Code of Virginia, General Assembly bills and DOLI announcements. Changes to law or assumptions come in as reviewed pull requests.
  • Not modeled: future legislation, a higher federal minimum, tipped wages, and training or youth exemptions. Projections decades out depend entirely on the inflation assumption.
